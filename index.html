About Us

Lady D Stores is a leading manufacturing enterprise based in Yola North, Adamawa State. Strategically located at 23 Brigade Mammy Market, Army Barracks, Jimeta, we have built our reputation on delivering quality manufactured products that meet the needs of our diverse clientele.

Our commitment to excellence, attention to detail, and understanding of the local market makes us the first choice for businesses and individuals seeking reliable manufacturing solutions. We combine modern production techniques with traditional Nigerian values of hard work and integrity.
